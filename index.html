About me

Who's behind the keyboard

I'm a technical lead at MTN Nigeria. Most of my work sits where product decisions meet system design: deciding what is worth building, then making sure the architecture can carry it.

Before MTN, at Enyata, I led the team that built a government cash-transfer disbursement platform end to end and designed its backend architecture. Systems that move public money teach you to think about correctness, auditability and failure modes before features.

I also run Sprout Labs, a software consulting agency, and I write about the engineering problems I find most interesting: payments, distributed systems and data modelling.
